Quarterly Results Summary (synthetic filing excerpt, for hackathon evaluation only)

[Page 3, Management Discussion]
The company delivered a strong quarter. Revenue increased 24% year-over-year, driven by
higher unit volumes in the core hardware segment and continued growth in subscription
services. Management attributes the acceleration to the launch of two new product lines
in the prior quarter and expanded distribution in international markets.

Operating margin held steady at 18%, in line with the prior year, as higher revenue offset
increased input costs from a temporary rise in component prices.

[Page 37, Notes to the Financial Statements — Restatement]
Following a review of revenue recognition timing for multi-year subscription contracts,
the company has restated prior-period figures. On a restated basis, revenue increased
17% year-over-year for the quarter, reflecting the deferral of a portion of subscription
revenue that had previously been recognized upfront. The restatement affects only the
timing of recognition and does not change total contract value. Management does not
believe the restatement is material to the full-year outlook.

[Page 41, Segment Detail]
The hardware segment grew 21% year-over-year. The subscription services segment grew
31% year-over-year on a reported basis; management has not yet disclosed a restated
figure for this segment specifically.
